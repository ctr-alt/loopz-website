About LOOPZ

Made for Android, designed around your space.

LOOPZ is an Android media app focused on giving users a clean place to download supported media, manage a personal library, browse offline, and make edits. The experience is designed to feel local-first and straightforward.

The product is intentionally clear about what it supports today: Instagram video/Reel downloading and X video downloading are currently supported. Other platforms may be evaluated for future updates.

What LOOPZ is built for

  • Downloading supported media from currently supported platforms.
  • Organizing files in a Media Library.
  • Browsing with an offline vertical video Feed.
  • Editing with Media Studio tools.
  • Managing active and completed downloads.
